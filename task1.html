Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences (ANITS)

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences (ANITS) is one of the leading autonomous engineering colleges in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. Established in 2001, the college is approved by AICTE, permanently affiliated with Andhra University, and accredited by NAAC and NBA. ANITS offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, technology, and management, with modern laboratories, digital classrooms, a well-stocked library, sports facilities, and Wi-Fi-enabled campus infrastructure. The institute focuses on quality education, research, innovation, skill development, and industry-oriented training. It has a dedicated placement cell that provides training and recruitment opportunities with leading companies, helping students build successful careers. ANITS is well known for its academic excellence, disciplined environment, experienced faculty, and strong emphasis on overall personality development.
